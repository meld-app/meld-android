# Privacy Policy — MELD for Android

**MELD for Android** operates **100% locally and offline** on your device.

1. **Zero Network Permissions**: MELD for Android does not request `android.permission.INTERNET`.
2. **Zero Cloud Uploads**: All PDFs, images, audio files, and spreadsheets are processed strictly in your device's local app cache (`context.cacheDir`).
3. **Zero Telemetry or Analytics**: No analytics SDKs, crash trackers, or advertising identifiers are bundled.
